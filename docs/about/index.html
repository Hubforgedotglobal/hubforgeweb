About HubForge

One name for practical MEL

HubForge Global Impact Network helps people measure what matters and act on it. The promise is simple. Practical MEL: free to start, fair-priced to scale. The line we work under is: Build evidence. Drive change.

How the name works

HubForge is the masterbrand. Every product carries "by HubForge", whether it is a free tool, a playbook, a community programme, or advisory work. You should not have to guess which organisation sits behind the thing you just opened.

The public site is organised in four parts: Tools, Playbooks, Community, and Advisory.

Who we are here for

  • NGOs, grassroots groups, and social enterprises
  • Evaluators and MERL professionals
  • Young and emerging evaluators, and students
  • Funders who want practical evidence, not a performance

MERL means monitoring, evaluation, research and learning. MEL means monitoring, evaluation and learning. M&E means monitoring and evaluation. We spell them out because the letters are not the point. The work is.

What we hold to

Evidence with Heart

Data should serve people.

Local Wisdom First

Communities know their context, so we listen and build with them.

Global Connections

Evaluators, organisations, and learners, linked across places.

Practical over Perfect

A plan you can run beats a plan that only looks finished.

Youth as Catalysts

Young and emerging evaluators belong in the work, not at the edge of it.
