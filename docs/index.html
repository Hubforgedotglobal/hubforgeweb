Questions

MEL, in plain language

What does practical MEL mean at HubForge?

Practical MEL means monitoring, evaluation and learning you can start without a big budget and grow when you are ready. HubForge offers free tools and playbooks, a community, and fair-priced advisory for organisations.

Which free monitoring and evaluation tools does HubForge offer?

Four free tools, each by HubForge. EvalCanvas is a monitoring and evaluation (M&E) planning platform where you build, learn, connect, and play. SamplingShala is a sampling hub with a wizard, calculators, and a statistics library. EvalStudio is a creative studio with MEL cards, stickers, memes, and workshop activities. EvalQR makes QR codes and short-link analytics.

Does HubForge offer M&E consulting and evaluation services?

Yes. HubForge Advisory is affordable monitoring, evaluation, research and learning (MERL) support for organisations. It is priced below for-profit firms, on a sliding scale by organisation size. Work includes evaluation design, MEL frameworks and theory of change, data review and dashboards, survey and sampling design, and capacity building and training. Email hubforgeglobal@gmail.com to talk. Prices are shared in that conversation, not on this page.

What is the difference between MEL, M&E, and MERL?

M&E means monitoring and evaluation. MEL means monitoring, evaluation and learning. MERL means monitoring, evaluation, research and learning. HubForge uses all three, and leads with practical MEL.
