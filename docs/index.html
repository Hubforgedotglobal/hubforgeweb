Questions, answered

MEL, made accessible to everyone

What is MEL (Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning)?

MEL stands for Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning, a practice that helps organizations track progress, measure results, and learn from evidence to improve their work. It builds on traditional M&E by adding a deliberate focus on learning and adaptation.

What free MEL tools does HubForge offer?

HubForge offers free MEL tools including EvalCanvas, an AI-supported MEL strategy platform; SamplingShala, a sampling design companion; a curated M&E Resource Hub; and EvalQR analytics. Most are free to use with no account required.

Does HubForge offer MEL training and capacity building?

Yes. HubForge Impact Lab provides volunteer-driven MEL and MERL training, tools, and hands-on support so grassroots and community organizations can build their evidence practice from the ground up.

Where can I find MEL and evaluation jobs?

The HubForge Impact Jobs Hub is a free, global board for monitoring, evaluation, research, technology, and social impact roles, with new opportunities published continuously.

How can I connect with other evaluators and MEL professionals?

EvalAtlas is HubForge's global ecosystem and networking platform connecting evaluators, NGOs, funders, youth, and field researchers in one inclusive space.

What is the difference between MEL, M&E, and MERL?

M&E means Monitoring and Evaluation. MEL adds Learning (Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning). MERL adds Research (Monitoring, Evaluation, Research and Learning). All describe how organizations use evidence to measure impact and improve programs.

Is HubForge a registered nonprofit?

Yes. HubForge Global Impact Network Foundation is a Section 8 not-for-profit registered in India (Darpan ID KAR/2025/0843199), serving changemakers and organizations worldwide.
