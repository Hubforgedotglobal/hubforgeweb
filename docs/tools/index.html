Tools for practical MEL

Four free products for monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL). Open one and start. When the work needs a person as well as a tool, talk to HubForge Advisory.

Free tools

Freeby HubForge

EvalCanvas

An M&E planning platform. Build, learn, connect, and play. Made for programme teams who need a monitoring and evaluation (M&E) plan they can actually use.

Open EvalCanvas (opens in a new tab)
Freeby HubForge

SamplingShala

A sampling hub with a wizard, calculators, and a statistics library. Use it to shape a sample, then check the design with someone who knows your context.

Open SamplingShala (opens in a new tab)
Freeby HubForge

EvalStudio

A creative studio with MEL cards, stickers, memes, and workshop activities. Useful when a session needs to feel human, not like a slide dump.

Open EvalStudio (opens in a new tab)
Freeby HubForge

EvalQR

QR codes and short-link analytics. Share a link in a room, a report, or a poster, and see what people open.

Open EvalQR (opens in a new tab)
