HubForge Advisory · by HubForge

Fair-priced help for the MEL you cannot do alone

HubForge Advisory is monitoring, evaluation, research and learning (MERL) support for organisations. It is priced below for-profit firms, on a sliding scale by organisation size. Practical MEL: free to start with the tools, fair-priced when you need us in the work.

Talk to us Start with free tools

Who it is for

Organisations that need evidence and do not want a process built for a large firm. That includes NGOs, grassroots groups, and social enterprises. Evaluators and MERL professionals are welcome to talk if they want a collaborator, not a pitch.

What we do

Services

Tell us the decision you need to make. We will say if we are the right people, and what a smaller first step could be.

Evaluation design

Questions, methods, and a plan that fits the programme you actually run.

MEL frameworks and theory of change

A monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL) framework, and a theory of change, written so a team can use them.

Data review and dashboards

A clear look at data you already have, and dashboards people will open more than once.

Survey and sampling design

Forms and samples that match the decision, not a template copied from somewhere else.

Capacity building and training

Sessions for teams who want to own their evidence after we leave.

How pricing works

Fees sit below typical for-profit consulting, and they move with the size of the organisation. A small grassroots group is not quoted like a large institution.

We do not list prices here. Write to us with a short note about your organisation and the work, and we will reply with what it would take.
